## v0.2.0 (2026-10-24)

- First process, [camara_hourly_ingestion](specs/camara_hourly_ingestion.md): hourly CSV files
  of the *Votações* dataset of the Câmara dos Deputados, from a flat landing directory to
  `camara.votacoes` in PostgreSQL. `ReadSource` selects the files above a watermark kept in the
  table's own `source_file` column, and `AppendOnly` appends the batch in one transaction.
- A preparation script, `prepare_landing.py`, lays out the landing: one file per hour, two
  years of history at first.
- A `docker compose` environment of two services, `postgres` and `spark`, with the commands to
  bring it up and run the job in the README.
- Gap GAP-001 in `LIB_GAPS.md`: a landing in year/month/day folders cannot be read.
